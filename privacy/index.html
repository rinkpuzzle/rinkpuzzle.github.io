Rink Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Rink is a casual puzzle game. No account is required for core play. We do not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising.

Gameplay and device storage

Gameplay progress, settings, hearts, coins, daily rewards, and records of delivered purchases are stored locally on your device. Gameplay can work offline. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is also saved in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can be restored after a reinstall or on another device signed in to the same Apple Account. We do not receive this copy. If iCloud is off, progress may be lost if you delete the app or clear its data.

Optional purchases

If you buy coins or hearts, Apple processes the payment. Rink does not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat helps validate and deliver purchases and may receive purchase receipts or transaction information, product identifiers, and an anonymous app user ID. Apple and RevenueCat process this information to provide purchases, prevent fraud, and resolve purchase issues.

Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Please do not send passwords or payment card details. The website hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to operate and secure the site. Rink does not include game advertising or gameplay analytics.

Retention and your choices

Local gameplay data remains on your device until removed. The iCloud copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's terms. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase information under their policies and legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, including California or the European Economic Area, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where applicable. You may also complain to your local privacy authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; some payment records are controlled by Apple or RevenueCat and may need to be requested from them. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email Masonfife@outlook.com. We may update this policy as Rink changes and will revise the effective date above.
